# HnsMatchAddons

| Plugin | Description |
| --- | --- |
| [HnsAiraccelerate](HnsAiraccelerate.sma) | Allows watchers to set `sv_airaccelerate` to `10` or `100` using chat commands. |
| [HnsAirBlockReturn](HnsAirBlockReturn.sma) | Detects blocks and air flashes performed by Terrorists against Counter-Terrorists and reports them in chat. In supported modes, it can prevent fall damage and move the Counter-Terrorist back along their saved trajectory. If a Counter-Terrorist dies from a fall after being blocked or air-flashed during a mix, the responsible player is shown in the kill feed.<br><br>**Cvars:**<br>`hns_airblock_return 1` — controls player rollback (`0` — disabled, `1` — enabled).<br>`hns_airblock_damage 35.0` — minimum fall damage required to trigger a rollback.<br>`hns_airblock_chat 2` — controls chat messages (`0` — disabled, `1` — successful rollbacks only, `2` — all detected blocks and air flashes).<br>`hns_airblock_killfeed 1` — shows the blocker or flasher in the kill feed (`0` — disabled, `1` — enabled). |
| [HnsAntiStopStub](HnsAntiStopStub.sma) | Removes the player's slowdown after a knife hit in warm-up modes. |
| [HnsBlockObservers](HnsBlockObservers.sma) | During an active mix in Boost mode, prevents spectators from watching the last surviving Terrorists. If the Terrorist team initially had more than three players, the restriction activates when two remain alive; if it had three players, it activates when one remains. Administrators with the `ADMIN_BAN` flag bypass the restriction. |
| [HnsDMRespawn](HnsDMRespawn.sma) | Extends the `DM` and `DM_1TT` modes. After being killed by another player, a respawned Counter-Terrorist is moved to a safe position near the place where they died. After getting a kill, a Terrorist temporarily becomes invulnerable and translucent, giving them time to escape.<br><br>**Cvars:**<br>`hns_dmresp_ct_deathtp 1` — moves a Counter-Terrorist to their death position after respawning (`0` — disabled, `1` — enabled).<br>`hns_dmresp_tt_time 2.0` — duration of the Terrorist's invulnerability and translucency in seconds.<br>`hns_dmresp_tt_alpha 120` — Terrorist visibility level (`0` — fully invisible, `255` — fully visible). |
| [HnsKillAssist](HnsKillAssist.sma) | Adds an assist system to the kill feed. The player who dealt the most damage to the victim receives the assist if their share is at least 40% of the total damage dealt. The kill feed displays `killer + assistant`, and the assistant is awarded one frag. |
| [HnsSpawnCT](HnsSpawnCT.sma) | During freeze time, allows Counter-Terrorists to press `E` to cycle through and teleport between CT spawn points. After teleporting, it checks the selected position and moves the player to a free spot if they become stuck. |
| [HnsVoteManager](HnsVoteManager.sma) | A voting manager for watchers. It supports custom polls, mode votes between `Boost` and `Skill`, and map votes. Maps are grouped into `Boost`, `Skill`, and nominated categories, allowing a watcher to select from 2 to 5 options. A tied map vote can be restarted, and the initiator can confirm the map change after a winner is determined.<br><br>**Commands:**<br>`/vote <question>` — starts a Yes/No poll.<br>`/votemode` — starts a vote between `Boost` and `Skill`.<br>`/votemap`, `/votemaps` — opens the map selection menu and starts a map vote. |
